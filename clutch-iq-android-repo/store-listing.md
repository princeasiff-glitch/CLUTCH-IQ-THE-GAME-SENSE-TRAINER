## Full description

Think fast. Play smarter. Clutch the win.

Clutch IQ is not a trivia app. You won't get questions about names, weapons or release dates. Every question is a real in-match moment: the final circle, an eco round, a lost objective, a teammate down. You read the situation, beat the clock, and make the call you would make in a real match.

HOW IT WORKS
• Read the moment: a short situation with a ticking timer
• Make the call: pick A, B, C or D before time runs out
• Learn why: see the coach pick, why the other options are weaker, and what every other player chose

FEATURES
• 70 decision scenarios across 9 packs, covering battle royale, tactical shooter, MOBA and strategy fundamentals, plus game-specific packs
• Daily Clutch: one scenario, the same for every player, every day
• Coach pick vs crowd pick: compare your thinking with everyone else's
• Skill stats: find out whether you lose on positioning, timing, economy, teamwork or resources
• XP and ranks from Bronze to Legend
• Daily streaks and a weekly leaderboard
• Share results with friends on WhatsApp or anywhere else
• Game-style music and sound effects, with a mute button
• Free and unlimited, with no ads

Five minutes a day is enough to sharpen your decisions. Think a coach pick is wrong? Tap "Disagree? Email us" and tell us why. We read every message, and game updates change the best play all the time.

Made by AFSR Games. Ideas, game requests or problems: afsrpublishing@gmail.com

Clutch IQ is an independent training app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any game publisher. Game names are used only to identify which game a scenario relates to.

> Listing tip: keep game titles (PUBG Mobile, Valorant, Free Fire and others) **out** of the title, short description and screenshots captions. Using other companies' trademarks in store metadata is a common reason for rejection. Using them inside the app with the disclaimer is fine.
