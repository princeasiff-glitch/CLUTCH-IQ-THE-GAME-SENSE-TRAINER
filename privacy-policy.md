# Clutch IQ Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 10 November 2026
**Live version:** https://clutch-iq.apper.so/privacy

Clutch IQ: The Game Sense Trainer ("Clutch IQ", "we", "us") is made by AFSR Games, part of AFSR Publishing. This policy explains what we collect when you use Clutch IQ on the web or on Android, and how we use it.

## Who we are
AFSR Publishing, Hyderabad, India. Website: https://afsrpublishing.store. Contact: afsrpublishing@gmail.com

## What we collect
- **Account details:** your name or display name and your email address, which you give us when you sign up.
- **Gameplay data:** the scenarios you play, the option you picked, whether it matched the coach pick, how long you took, the XP you earned and the date played.
- **Settings stored on your device only:** your sound on/off choice and whether you have seen the intro animation. These never leave your device.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, files, payment details or device advertising ID.

## How we use it
- To run your account and sign you in.
- To show your stats, streaks, rank and history.
- To show the leaderboard. Other players can see your display name, XP and accuracy there.
- To show the crowd split on each scenario. This uses everyone's answers combined and never names anyone.
- To reply when you email us.

## Sharing
We do not sell or rent your data. The app runs on the Apper platform, which stores the data for us as a service provider. We only share data beyond that if the law requires it.

When you use the Share button, your phone's share menu sends what you choose to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp.

## Advertising
Clutch IQ has no ads at the moment. If we add ads later, they will be gaming-related only, and we will update this policy and the Play Store listing before they go live.

## Security and retention
All data travels over HTTPS. We keep your data while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and gameplay data within 30 days.

## Your choices
- Turn music and sounds off at any time from the menu.
- Ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing afsrpublishing@gmail.com.
- Delete your account: https://clutch-iq.apper.so/delete-account

## Children
Clutch IQ is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete the account.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the in-app page.
